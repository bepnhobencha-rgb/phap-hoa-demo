About Phap Hoa Monastery

A Buddhist monastery dedicated to cultivating compassion, wisdom, and mindful living within the community.

Who we are

Phap Hoa Monastery is a Buddhist monastery dedicated to cultivating compassion, wisdom, and mindful living within the community. Rooted in the teachings of the Buddha, our monastery serves as a spiritual home for individuals and families seeking peace, understanding, and inner transformation.

We offer regular Dharma teachings, meditation practice, cultural celebrations, youth programs, and community service initiatives. Our mission is to preserve the richness of Buddhist tradition while making its wisdom accessible and relevant to modern life in Canada.

Phap Hoa Monastery welcomes people of all backgrounds, cultures, and faith traditions. Whether you are exploring Buddhism for the first time or continuing your spiritual journey, our doors are open to you.

Our story

2013 · Burnaby

Founded

Phap Hoa Monastery was founded by Venerable Nun Minh An in 2013 at 3730 Boundary Road, Burnaby. Established with the aspiration to create a spiritual sanctuary for Buddhist practice, the monastery became a place of refuge for the Vietnamese Buddhist community and those seeking peace and guidance.

2023 · Surrey

Moving to Surrey

In 2023, in order to expand and further develop its religious and community activities, Phap Hoa Monastery relocated to 18847 80 Avenue, Surrey. This new location allows the monastery to better serve its growing community and to continue its mission of spiritual cultivation and compassionate service.
