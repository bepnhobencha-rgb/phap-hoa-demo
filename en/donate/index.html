Future development projects

Master Plan of Phap Hoa Monastery

We humbly invite you to become part of this meaningful undertaking. Every contribution, whether large or small, brings us one step closer to realizing this vision.

Foundation phase · Project 1

Septic System Upgrade

The current system was designed for a smaller number of visitors. With increasing attendance during Sunday services, retreats, and large events such as Vesak, Ullambana Day, and Mid-Autumn Festival, the monastery must improve the septic system to ensure it can safely and efficiently support the growing community.

  • Meet city and environmental regulations
  • Support larger gatherings and community events
  • Improve sanitation and safety for all visitors
  • Prepare the monastery for future development and expansion

A properly upgraded septic system is a foundational step that allows the monastery to responsibly expand parking area.

Project 2

Parking Expansion & New Entrance

A new parking area with a dedicated entrance and exit from 188 Street and 80 Avenue is planned to better accommodate visitors and improve traffic flow. The current parking area in front of the Buddha Hall and residential building will be transformed into a serene spiritual landscape, including Zen gardens, a bell tower, and a drum tower — allowing the temple grounds to fully reflect its sacred atmosphere. This project will also enhance safety, by reducing vehicle movement in areas where the community gathers, walks, and practices.

Project 3

Indoor Kitchen & Dining Hall

Currently, the monastery’s kitchen and dining area are located outdoors, creating challenges for volunteers and visitors—especially during colder seasons and for seniors. In addition, the kitchen relies on temporary propane gas systems, which present fire safety concerns and are not suitable for long-term use. Phap Hoa Monastery plans to build an indoor kitchen and dining hall with proper facilities, ventilation, and fire-safe cooking systems to ensure a safer and more reliable environment. This project will support our Sunday vegetarian meals, community gatherings, and cultural events in a space that is comfortable, safe, and welcoming.

The Master Plan

The master plan reflects our vision for a traditional Buddhist monastery featuring a Main Buddha Hall, monastic residences, meditation gardens, community gathering spaces, and facilities that will support spiritual cultivation, education, cultural preservation, and compassionate service.

This project is more than the construction of buildings. It is the creation of a place where people can discover peace, develop wisdom, practice compassion, and connect with the timeless teachings of the Buddha.

Your generosity will help establish a lasting refuge for monastics, practitioners, families, youth, and all who seek guidance on the path of awakening.

Why your support matters

Phap Hoa Monastery continues to serve the community through Dharma teachings, weekly services, youth programs, cultural celebrations, and charitable activities. Your generous support helps us:

  • Maintain and improve the temple facilities
  • Offer free Sunday vegetarian meals
  • Support community outreach and charity work
  • Expand future development and spiritual programs

Your generosity supports the continuation of the Buddha’s teachings and helps sustain a peaceful refuge for present and future generations.

With gratitude and blessings.
