Since 2013

A spiritual home for the community

Phap Hoa Monastery was founded by Venerable Nun Minh An in 2013 in Burnaby and relocated to Surrey in 2023 to expand its religious and community activities. We offer regular Dharma teachings, meditation practice, cultural celebrations, youth programs, and community service initiatives.

About the monastery
Future development

Master Plan of Phap Hoa Monastery

A traditional Buddhist monastery with a Main Buddha Hall, monastic residences, meditation gardens and community spaces. First steps: a septic system upgrade, parking expansion with a new entrance, and an indoor kitchen and dining hall.
